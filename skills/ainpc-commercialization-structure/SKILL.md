# ainpc-commercialization-structure

## When to use

Use this skill when a game has AI NPC companions and the task requires:

- separating one-time character sales from account-level seasonal Pass sales;
- designing a recurring active-accumulation loop for small and mid spenders;
- designing deep-spend systems for high spenders without raising Pass prices;
- reviewing rewards for visibility, persistence, and system-loop fit;
- using AI capabilities to create deliverable digital works rather than selling model calls, memory capacity, or battle strength.

## Steps

1. **Define the free experience boundary.** Keep default AI companion dialogue, basic voice interaction, teamwork, emotional response, memory, relationship growth, and competitive capability available without a paywall. Character purchase changes the character identity carrying the experience; it does not raise model quality or battle power.
2. **Separate the three commercial systems.**
   - Active accumulation: a free, cross-day, universal continuity system; a seasonal Pass is only one optional reward layer for small and mid spenders.
   - Subscription: recurring functional rights and out-of-match model capability; use a low-price trial/entry product only as a clearly bounded acquisition exception.
   - Deep spend: affection/relationship presentation, digital tipping, and character-exclusive skins for high and top spenders.
3. **Build the active-accumulation system before choosing the reward wrapper.** Let all players accumulate activity across days (for example, 2 matches today plus 2 tomorrow equals 4 accumulated matches). Use existing game events and daily/weekly/seasonal activity; a Pass may expose subscriber-only rewards on the same progress, but it is not the definition of active accumulation and should not replace the universal base track.
4. **Use partner-targeted nodes.** A Pass node grants a final digital reward or a bounded adaptation right. The player selects one owned AINPC, and the final asset is permanently attached to that character's collection. The Pass must support any owned AINPC through a shared adaptation standard.
5. **Keep Pass scope small/mid spender.** Use standard and advanced Pass tiers only. Do not create whale/ultra-whale Pass tiers or put multi-character co-performance and annual identity systems into the Pass.
6. **Make deep-spend rewards role-specific and high-value.** Use three lines:
   - affection system: free relationship growth, paid high-grade commemorative works;
   - tipping system: paid digital gifts with explicit character response works, not paid affection or paid priority;
   - character-exclusive skins: visual, performance, and event-triggered reaction packages that do not alter AI ability or combat outcomes.
7. **Embed AI as a capability layer, not a fourth monetization category.**
   - Pass: stage event cards, seasonal chronicles, dynamic memory cards;
   - subscription: out-of-match review, long-form organization, multimodal creation;
   - deep spend: high-grade highlight videos, gift-thank-you works, multi-character co-performance works, AI-responsive skin presentation.
8. **Sell final works, not permissions.** Do not sell generation counts, reroll counts, editor access, model quotas, memory capacity, or response priority. State the exact deliverable, its contents, display entry points, and permanent storage location.
9. **Close the seasonal loop.** The loop should be: normal gameplay → real event accumulation → immediate stage event card → season-end finished work → equip/display in lobby, result, profile, or companion space → permanent character/account collection → next season.
10. **Review every reward.** Verify that it is digital-only, visibly usable, persistent after the season, assigned to the correct payer tier, and not duplicated by the deep-spend system.

## Pitfalls

- Do not confuse active accumulation with the Pass. Active accumulation must serve all players and provide cross-day continuity; the Pass is only a paid reward layer for small and mid spenders.
- Do not assume active users are subscribers. Active accumulation creates the sustained usage scene; subscription conversion is a later opportunity, and subscription rewards should feed activity back.
- Do not sell “smarter AI”, more memory, better combat decisions, or paid emotional preference.
- Do not describe an adaptation permission as the product headline; show the final character asset in preview and purchase copy.
- Do not put multi-character co-performance, annual collections, or owner/producer identity into the regular Pass.
- Do not use physical goods or fulfillment rewards when the design scope is digital-only.
- Do not sell AI generation as a metered utility. Deliver finished chronicles, cards, videos, or co-performance works.
- Do not create an AI-only daily grind disconnected from the game’s existing activity system.
- Keep subscription acquisition/trial rules explicit. If character ownership must precede all paid content, convert the trial into a post-purchase advanced-function trial; otherwise label pre-purchase trial as an explicit exception that does not grant ownership.

## Verification

Before delivery, check:

- [ ] Character, seasonal Pass, subscription, and deep-spend systems have separate roles.
- [ ] Pass has only small/mid-spender tiers.
- [ ] Deep-spend lines explicitly include affection presentation, tipping, and character-exclusive skins.
- [ ] Every AI reward is a named final digital work with a concrete display and storage location.
- [ ] No reward is physical, competitive, memory-capacity-based, or model-quality-based.
- [ ] Every season reward has acquire → use → in-season feedback → permanent collection steps.
- [ ] New AINPCs can enter the current season through the shared adaptation standard.
